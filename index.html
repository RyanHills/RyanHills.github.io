About Clixel Games

We’re an independent studio based in Eastbourne, East Sussex (UK). We craft atmospheric, systems-driven experiences with approachable scope and a strong sense of place. Our pipeline emphasises rapid prototyping, player feedback, and clean, readable design.

Clixel Games is currently focused on Shadows Below, with additional prototypes in R&D. Want to collaborate, preview builds, or chat? We’d love to hear from you.
